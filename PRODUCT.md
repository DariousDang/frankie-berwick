# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Local Berwick residents, workers, families, and shoppers looking for a quick coffee, an easy takeaway meal, or a relaxed breakfast and lunch stop at Eden Rise Village. Visitors are usually choosing on a phone and need the menu, opening hours, location, and a tap-to-call action immediately.

## Product Purpose

Present Frankie’s Berwick as a friendly local café and takeaway, make the supplied food and drink menus easy to scan, and turn casual visits into calls and in-person orders. Success means visitors quickly understand what Frankie’s serves, find a suitable choice, and know exactly how to visit or call.

## Brand Personality

Upbeat, generous, familiar. The voice should feel like a cheerful neighbourhood counter: direct, welcoming, and full of appetite without becoming loud or gimmicky.

## Anti-references

Avoid fine-dining formality, generic beige café templates, black-and-gold luxury styling, delivery-app clutter, oversized icon-card grids, and imagery or claims that imply a polished table-service restaurant rather than an approachable café and takeaway.

## Design Principles

1. Put appetite first with decisive food imagery and bold menu highlights.
2. Make the practical details effortless to find on a phone.
3. Let the visual identity feel local and human, not corporate.
4. Keep prices, dietary markers, surcharges, and hours clear and honest.
5. Use the supplied menu as the source of truth for food and drink content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA with clear focus states, strong text contrast, touch-friendly controls, semantic structure, reduced-motion support, and plain-language dietary labels. Avoid relying on colour alone to communicate menu attributes.
